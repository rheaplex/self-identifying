A PostScript Quine that signs itself with a cryptographic key generated from its own source code.

# Assertion

The code renders an assertion, giving itself a voice and seeking to engage with the artworld game of deciding the status of art.

# Bitmap

That code renders the cryptographic hash of itself as a simple monochrome bitmap, producing another level of identity, presentation, and aesthetics.

# Blue

This code renders a blue square, a least-object of aesthetics, to establish itself as both art and artist under the identity of its cryptographic key.

# Checks

Invoking op art and non-cryptoeconomic games, the program's code renders a monochrome chequerboard grid.

# Circle

In generating the image of a circle that code produces an aesthetic and graphical primitive - something that serves as art under the attention invited by the program's operation.

# Critique
